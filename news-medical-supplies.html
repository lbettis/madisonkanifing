Medical supplies arrive in The Gambia

July 27, 2018·Banjul, The Gambia

We received the good news that the container of medical equipment and supplies we sent to The Gambia arrived safely, and the items are being distributed to hospitals.

The shipment was received by the Director of Logistics at the Central Medical Stores on behalf of the Ministry of Health and Social Welfare. Items have already begun to be dispatched to Edward Francis Small Teaching Hospital (EFSTH), Serekunda Hospital and the Bundung Maternal Ward, with more facilities to follow.

What was donated

  • Surgery supplies 122
  • Patient care supplies 107
  • Intravenous supplies 71
  • Adult respiratory supplies 48
  • Bandages and dressings 43
  • Orthopaedic supplies 26
  • Gloves 20
  • Clinical supplies 19
  • Diabetes supplies 10
  • Obstetrics and gynaecology 9
  • Tubing 9
  • Gastro-intestinal supplies 7
  • Electrodes 6
  • Medical equipment 6
  • Paediatrics and neonatal 6
  • Disposable underwear and pads 4
  • Radiology supplies 3
  • Dental supplies 2
  • ENT supplies 2
  • Total cartons 520

Thank you to everyone who contributed their effort and support to make this possible.
