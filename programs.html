What We Do

Our programs

Promoting collaboration and capacity building in health, agriculture, civic and economic development, cultural affairs and education.

01  ·  Education

Education

Initiating and supporting academic exchanges, communication, friendship and sustained learning between students, teachers and faculty — from elementary through university — through seminars, workshops, conferences, scholarships and exchanges, along with book, school-supply and clothing drives, literacy campaigns, teacher training, and volunteer and service projects.

02  ·  Health

Health

Strengthening healthcare in Kanifing and across The Gambia by collecting and shipping medical supplies and equipment to hospitals and clinics, encouraging exchanges between health professionals, and helping young people pursue careers in healthcare through the Fatoumatta Jallow Memorial Scholarship Fund.

03  ·  Culture

Cultural Exchange

Celebrating the unique cultural and artistic diversity of both cities through heritage and art fairs, cultural exchanges, learning about the land, people and life of our sister city, screenings of educational and cultural films with facilitated discussion, and other activities that build appreciation for the rich cultural lives of both communities.

04  ·  Agriculture

Agriculture & Clean Energy

Promoting collaboration and capacity building in sustainable agricultural practices and inclusive growth programs in clean energy. A carefully planned approach to integrating sustainable agriculture and clean energy can help reduce inefficiencies such as post-harvest loss and give small farmers an edge.

05  ·  Civic & Economic

Civic & Economic Development

Establishing and supporting sustainable financial empowerment and small-business endeavors, and forging collaboration with local civic organizations to advance economic development and civic leadership in both cities.
