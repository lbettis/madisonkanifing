Scholarship

Fatoumatta Jallow Memorial Scholarship Fund

Helping students in The Gambia pursue higher education in the healthcare field.

Donations Needed

Invest in the next generation of Gambian healthcare professionals

The Madison–Kanifing Sister City Committee presents the Fatoumatta Jallow Memorial Scholarship Fund. Funds donated support high school students in The Gambia as they pursue higher education in the healthcare field — strengthening communities and honoring Fatoumatta's memory.

Every contribution makes a difference, and all donations are tax deductible.

Ways to give

Give securely online through PayPal, or mail a check to our post office box.

By check
Payable to: Madison–Kanifing Sister City

Donate with PayPal
