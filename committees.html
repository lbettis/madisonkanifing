How We Work

Our committees

Four volunteer committees plan, coordinate and deliver the work of the Madison–Kanifing Sister City Committee (MKSC).

Planning Committee

Chair: Fatou Ceesay
01

Members

  • Ummu Drammeh
  • Adrienne White
  • Zainabu Kooistra
  • Susan Pastor

Responsibilities

  1. Plan, develop, coordinate and evaluate the committee's projects and programs.
  2. Strategically engage local partners to identify areas of collaboration and opportunities that positively impact the committee.
  3. Coordinate with the Secretary to design and document an annual schedule of events and programs.
  4. Develop short- and long-term strategic plans for MKSC in consultation with the Sister City Committee.

Madison–Kanifing Liaison Committee

Chair: Musa Jallow
02

Members

  • Zainabou Cole
  • Laity Joof
  • Jerreh Kujabi

Responsibilities

  1. Work closely with international partners and stakeholders to identify needs and strategic areas of collaboration.
  2. Develop a framework to coordinate successful program implementation.
  3. Define clear milestones and monitor the progress of all international projects and programs.
  4. Advise MKSC on potential travel advisories for the regions where we operate.

Outreach Committee

Chair: Nyima Jabang
03

Members

  • Julie Ndong
  • Amadou Janneh
  • Ismaila Drammeh
  • Jalika Ceesay

Responsibilities

  1. Coordinate and distribute brochures and promotional materials about MKSC and its activities.
  2. Support volunteer awareness campaigns as needed.
  3. Identify methods for, and help maintain, regular contact with the public.
  4. Provide insight and advice on public relations and communication.

Technology Committee

Chair: Leon Bettis
04

Members

  • Ismael Turner
  • Jerreh Kujabi

Responsibilities

  1. Provide guidance, feedback and direction on technology-related matters.
  2. Design, develop and maintain a functional website for the committee.
  3. Maintain and promote the committee's web presence through social media.
